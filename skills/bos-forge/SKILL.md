# Forge (BOS)

> Draft generated by `bos skills draft` from `docs/glossary/forge.md` and the core's `forge` model.
> Grow it by hand: it is never overwritten.

## What it is

The active workspace, the equivalent of a desk. It holds only projects that are
open and under active development. If something is in the forge, it is being
worked on.

## Where

`__WORKSHOP/forge/`

## Handling

`bos workshop status` reports the forge with every scope and project in it,
and the findings about them (reads only). New work goes to
`forge/<scope>/<project>`; `bos workshop bootstrap --dryRun` shows what is missing.
