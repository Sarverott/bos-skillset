# Craftbook (BOS)

> Draft generated by `bos skills draft` from `docs/glossary/craftbook.md` and the core's `craftbook` model.
> Grow it by hand: it is never overwritten.

## What it is

The workshop's knowledge area: drafts, notes, recipes and reusable procedures.
The old scrapbook was a synchronized notebook that gathered notes from
projects, shemes and throwboxes. The craftbook continues that role.

## Where

`__WORKSHOP/craftbook/`

## Submodules

- `scrapnotes` → `scrapnotes/`

## Handling

`bos workshop status` reports the craftbook (reads only). Notes and drafts not yet
promoted to documents go to `craftbook/scrapnotes/`. [`TODO`] other procedures.
